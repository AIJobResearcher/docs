[Architecture Overview](./architecture-overview.md) §1.

## 1. Stages

| # | Stage | Scope | Subdomain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Automatic vacancy catalogue updates | The vacancy list is refreshed in automatic mode together with AI | Vacancy Management, AI & Parsing |
| 2 | Core job search features | Job search, replies and messages, handled together with AI | Job Search & CRM |
| 3 | AI recommendations and advanced analytics | Recommendations for the job seeker in different cases, advanced reply analytics | Job Search & CRM, AI & Parsing |
| 4 | Learning centre | Learning centre driven by the requirements of employers: courses, real-time interview support, all with AI | Learning Management |
